Tech is now 6-0 and bowl-bound. It was my first photographing football, and experiencing Bobby Dodd from the field for an entire game was incredible. I got to feel the energy, intensity, and chaos of a live college football game up close. 

I got there early and had the chance to capture players during warm-ups, including quarterback Haynes King and wide receiver Jamal Haynes, two of the team’s standout performers. During this time, I also tested out my new Nikon AF-S DX Nikkor 55-200mm lens, still getting used to its feel.

When the game started, I found myself drawn to the moments when players celebrated, running off or walking away from the crowd. It allowed me to isolate them and cut out distractions. I also focused on side-profile shots, especially when players lined up for the snap, capturing tension and focus in a single frame. 

But it wasn’t just about the players. College football is a spectacle so I wanted to capture that too. From the roaring crowd to the marching band, Buzz, the Ramblin’ Wreck, cheerleaders, and gold rush dancers, every corner of Bobby Dodd was energetic.

![Alt text](/images/blog/band.jpg)


During a timeout, one of the players even called me over for a group picture, which is featured as the cover of this post. It was surreal to be that close to the team and feel like a part of the group, even if just for a moment. 

![Alt text](/images/blog/players.jpg)

One of my favorite moments was snapping a close-up of head coach Brent Key and seeing him interacting with the referees from just five feet away. 

![Alt text](/images/blog/key.jpg)

As the game wrapped up, I stayed on the field to capture the human side of football. coaches from both teams exchanging handshakes, players from each side taking pictures, and players taking selfies with fans or giving them their gloves or towel before heading to the locker room. 

Navigating the crowded sidelines is still a skill I’m learning. Positioning yourself among moving players, coaches, and photographers requires predicting plays before they happen. But there's something special about witnessing the game not just as a fan, but as someone documenting every play. Georgia Tech’s 6-0 record isn’t just luck; it’s a success story I’m lucky enough to capture through my photos, and I can’t wait to do it again.
